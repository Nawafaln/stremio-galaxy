# Galaxy Anime release-year filter (community modification, GPL-3.0)

Release year / سنة الإنتاج appears beside Genres on every Galaxy Anime discovery catalog. Select a year to apply it immediately; All removes only the year and keeps genres/search. Genre changes likewise retain the year. Filter changes clear skip so paging starts again. The control follows the Galaxy interface locale (Arabic/English), uses the Galaxy palette and a keyboard-accessible native select. Disable it with yearEnabled:false in filters-config.js and restart.

The year means the first release/start year of that anime entry or season, never its ending year. Years range from 1900 through the current year plus two, generated by the local server (/years.json) so the list advances automatically. Unavailable dates are excluded only while year filtering is active.

Verified primary documentation: https://kitsu.docs.apiary.io/reference/posts (Anime seasonYear filter). Actual Kitsu request filter[seasonYear]=2024 with filter[categories]=isekai returned 2024 startDate values and titles including KonoSuba 3 and Mushoku Tensei II Part 2. Popular/airing use that paginated endpoint with existing genre/category AND filters and their original sort/status; results also verify startDate locally. The existing isekai.cjs catalog converter is shared, preserving Kitsu movie/series IDs and poster metadata; it never labels Kitsu scores as IMDb ratings.

Trending/search keep their existing bounded upstream sets, check releaseInfo's first year, combine genres and then slice pages. A title that began in 2023 and ended in 2024 is excluded from a 2024 filter. Empty results in trending/search mean no matches in the upstream set, not a global claim that no such anime exists. Source date/category coverage may be incomplete.
